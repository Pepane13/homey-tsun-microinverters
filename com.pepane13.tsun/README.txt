Keep an eye on your solar roof from Homey. This app connects to your TSUN micro-inverter (validated on the TSOL-MX3000D) directly over your local network, without the TSUN cloud, and brings its production into Homey Energy, panel by panel, every 30 seconds. It learns how each of your panels usually behaves, including the shadows of the trees and roofs around you, so it only warns you when a panel really has a problem, and it can forecast tomorrow's sunshine on your roof to help you plan your consumption.

The app only reads data and never sends any command to the inverter. Add the device during daytime, when the inverter is awake, with the IP address and serial number of its Wi-Fi logger. This app is not affiliated with TSUN or Solarman.
